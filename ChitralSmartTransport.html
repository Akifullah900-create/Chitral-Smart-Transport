📚 Chitral AI Knowledge Base

The assistant knows the locations and transport-planning information configured for this prototype.

🗺️ Locations: Chitral Town, Seenlasht, Ayun, Drosh, Booni, Mastuj, Garum Chashma, Khot, Rech and Shagram.
👥 Passenger planning: route matching, preferred departure time, vehicle preference, seat requests, driver acceptance, confirmed bookings and private/shared ride alternatives.
💰 Configured fares: Seenlasht ↔ Chitral Rs 150 • Ayun ↔ Chitral Rs 250 • Drosh ↔ Chitral Rs 350 • Booni ↔ Chitral Rs 700. Other configured routes have their own prototype fare values.
📊 Regional context: PBS Census-2023 reports 278,328 people in Lower Chitral and 195,528 in Upper Chitral. It also reports very low population density in both districts, which is relevant context for distributed transport planning.
⚠️ Data transparency: Census figures are source-backed. Route demand estimates in this prototype are demonstration assumptions and should not be presented as official passenger counts.

📈 AI Demand Prediction — Chitral Region

Uses route-specific prototype demand profiles, time windows, seasonal planning factors, expected passengers and available capacity.

🧠 What the prototype AI considers
🕐 Time of day
Morning, midday, evening and off-peak factors.
🌦️ Season
Conservative winter/summer planning factors.
💺 Capacity
Active vehicle seats plus additional capacity entered by the operator.
🛣️ Route profile
Different prototype baselines for local, southern and Upper Chitral corridors.
Model type: hybrid. The app computes demand features (route baseline, time window, season, live seats, capacity gap) and a live large language model (Groq, OpenAI GPT-OSS 120B) reasons over them to give the demand level and a recommendation. Baselines are synthetic demo data, not official ridership; real booking history would improve accuracy.
