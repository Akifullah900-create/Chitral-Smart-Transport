📚 Chitral AI Knowledge Base

The assistant knows the locations and transport-planning information configured for this prototype.

🗺️ Locations: Chitral Town, Seenlasht, Ayun, Drosh, Booni, Mastuj, Garum Chashma, Khot, Rech and Shagram.
🚗 Vehicle intelligence: Corolla and Suzuki are treated as 4-seat vehicles; Coaster as 14 seats; Local Bus as 24 seats. The system checks capacity before a trip starts and limits passenger seat requests accordingly.
🧭 Driver intelligence: The system considers route, vehicle type, offered ride type, available seats, departure time and current GPS position when displaying active transport options.
💡 Booking intelligence: Preferred ride type is checked first. If it is unavailable but another compatible ride type is active on the same route, the passenger is offered that alternative instead of receiving only a generic no-result message.
💰 Fare intelligence: Route base fares are combined with the selected ride type. Public is intended as the lower-cost option, Shared as a mid-level option, and Private as a higher-cost dedicated option. These are prototype pricing rules and can be changed by the operator.
📍 GPS intelligence: Driver GPS updates the vehicle's latitude, longitude and timestamp. In this browser prototype the shared state is local to the browser; a real deployment needs a backend/realtime database for cross-device live tracking.
🧠 Demand intelligence: The demand assistant combines a route baseline, time window, seasonal planning factor, expected passengers and available capacity to produce a planning level and capacity recommendation. It is a rule-based demonstration, not a trained machine-learning forecast.
🛡️ Safety/data note: GPS, fares, demand estimates and route information should be validated with local operators and real transport data before operational deployment.
👥 Passenger planning: route matching, preferred departure time, vehicle preference, seat requests, driver acceptance, confirmed bookings and private/shared ride alternatives.
💰 Configured fares: Seenlasht ↔ Chitral Rs 150 • Ayun ↔ Chitral Rs 250 • Drosh ↔ Chitral Rs 350 • Booni ↔ Chitral Rs 700. Other configured routes have their own prototype fare values.
🧾 Fare intelligence: The AI can explain how route base fares and ride-type multipliers work. Public uses 0.70×, Shared 0.85× and Private 1.35× of the configured route base in this prototype. The result is a demonstration fare, not a regulated tariff. Operators should verify fares against local costs, distance, vehicle operating expenses and approved transport rules.
🚗 Vehicle intelligence: Suzuki and Corolla are 4-seat vehicles, Coaster has 14 seats and Local Bus has 24 seats in this prototype. AI can compare capacity, ride type suitability, passenger seat limits and typical use: compact/economy trips for Suzuki, small private or group trips for Corolla, medium groups for Coaster and high-capacity public transport for Local Bus.
🪪 Driver licence authentication: The driver form stores a licence/ID reference for demonstration. A real AI verification system should check the licence through an authorized government/licensing service, validate identity and licence status, expiry date and permitted vehicle category, and send uncertain cases to a human operator. The current offline/browser prototype does not perform official licence verification.
🔐 Driver authentication & safety: Driver approval should be a separate verification step before a driver can accept passengers. Recommended production checks include identity verification, licence validity, vehicle registration/fitness, insurance where applicable, and operator approval. AI can flag missing or inconsistent information, but it should not declare a licence legally valid without an authoritative verification source.
📊 Regional context: PBS Census-2023 reports 278,328 people in Lower Chitral and 195,528 in Upper Chitral. It also reports very low population density in both districts, which is relevant context for distributed transport planning.
⚠️ Data transparency: Census figures are source-backed. Route demand estimates in this prototype are demonstration assumptions and should not be presented as official passenger counts.

📈 AI Demand Prediction — Chitral Region

Uses route-specific prototype demand profiles, time windows, seasonal planning factors, expected passengers and available capacity.

🧠 What the prototype AI considers
🕐 Time of day
Morning, midday, evening and off-peak factors.
🌦️ Season
Conservative winter/summer planning factors.
💺 Capacity
Active vehicle seats plus additional capacity entered by the operator.
🛣️ Route profile
Different prototype baselines for local, southern and Upper Chitral corridors.
💰 Fare planning
AI can compare route base fares with Public, Shared and Private pricing rules and identify where operator review may be needed.
🚗 Vehicle planning
AI can suggest a vehicle class by expected passengers and configured capacity: 4-seat Suzuki/Corolla, 14-seat Coaster or 24-seat Local Bus.
🪪 Driver verification
Demand planning should count only approved drivers in a production system. Licence and vehicle documents require authoritative verification.
Model type: transparent rule-based demonstration. It is not a trained machine-learning forecast and does not use live official ridership data.
